The Lore

Originating from the Shibuya underground, 0CROSS is an uncompromising audio project driven by a completely hands-on approach. The operation runs top to bottom, covering beat production, lyric writing, and live show logistics.

Powered by modern hardware including the Teenage Engineering EP-133 K.O. II and the Solid State Logic 2 MKII, the sound is a collision of glitch aesthetics, precision lyricism, and heavy bass lines.
